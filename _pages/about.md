I am a first-year PhD student in Economics at both Sciences Po and Vilnius University (cotutelle). I hold an MSc (Honours) in Econometrics from the University of Amsterdam and a BSc in Quantitative Economics from Vilnius University.

My research interests are in topics of international trade, geoeconomics, and macroeconomics. My current work focuses on how economic agents adjust to geoeconomic fragmentation, including trade disruptions, sanctions, and shifts in global production networks. More recently, I am working on developing a theoretical model of how forward-looking firms adjust in anticipation to potential future shocks.
